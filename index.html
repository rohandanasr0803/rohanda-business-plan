01 / THE PROPOSITIONTHE BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT VISION
A COMMERCIAL-FIRST APPROACH

Beyond business
development as usual.

In architectural consultancy, commercial growth begins well before an RFP. The proposed approach combines early project intelligence, access to decision-makers, tailored multidisciplinary positioning and disciplined conversion.

The goal is not simply to generate more contacts. It is to create a repeatable process for turning relevant market access into qualified consultancy opportunities and sustainable business.

03 / MARKET FOCUSSELECTIVE REGIONAL GROWTH
WHERE GROWTH MAY EMERGE

Opportunity-led.
Not geography-led.

Saudi Arabia and Oman form the initial assessment focus. Every market and target account remains subject to project maturity, competitive position, regulatory eligibility and commercial feasibility.

COMMERCIAL DISCIPLINE

Named organizations are prospective research accounts, not confirmed opportunities, tenders or existing DCI PLUS relationships. Professional licensing and procurement requirements must be verified for each opportunity.
